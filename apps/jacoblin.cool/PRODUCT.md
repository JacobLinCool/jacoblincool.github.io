## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Jacob Lin's personal website presents research, publications, and engineering work, with an AI assistant for exploring that work. A blog gives Jacob a place to publish whenever he wants to write.

## Operating Context

Blog posts are Markdown files committed to the repository and published with the website deployment. The author explicitly chose this workflow. Drafts can be previewed locally before publication.

## Evidence on Hand

The shared agent knowledge registry contains the site's publication and project records. Google Scholar identifies publications; publisher and arXiv pages verify titles, authors, venues, and summaries. Article content is supplied by the author.
